Tap a tile to pick it up, tap another tile or an empty slot to move it, or drag. On your turn take from the pile or from the discard on your left, then discard to your right, or hit Finish with your last tile when the other 14 make sets. Runs are one color in a row (12-13-1 is fine, 13-1-2 isn't), groups are one number in 3–4 colors, or go out with 7 pairs. Okey is the tile one above the indicator and is wild; the ★ jokers stand in for the okey's own value. Points: finish 2, seven pairs 4, finishing by throwing the okey doubles it.
